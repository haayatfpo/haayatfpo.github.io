Overview

Farm machinery is essential for timely sowing and harvesting, but ownership is beyond reach for most smallholders. Haayat FPO operates a shared equipment rental program — giving members affordable access to tractors, rotavators, seed drills, and combine harvesters during critical farm operations without the burden of loans or maintenance costs.

Serving farmers across Sehore district from our hub at Bhairunda (Nasrullaganj) — Village Nehrugaon, Post Rithwar, Pin 466331.

What We Offer

  • Tractor and rotavator rental for land preparation
  • Seed drills and planting equipment for precise sowing
  • Combine harvesters during peak harvest windows
  • Operator-assisted deployment where available
  • Member-priority booking during peak season

Member Benefits

  • No large capital investment required for machinery ownership
  • Timely farm operations — sow and harvest at the right window
  • Reduced dependency on expensive private contractors
  • Maintained equipment ready when your crop needs it

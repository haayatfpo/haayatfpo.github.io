Sharbati Gehu (Wheat)

Haayat FPO supports cultivation, aggregation, and marketing of authentic Sharbati Gehu — the renowned GI-tagged wheat variety of Madhya Pradesh.

Haayat FPO is proud to support authentic GI-tagged Sharbati Gehu from Madhya Pradesh — a premium wheat variety recognized for its quality and heritage.

Overview

Sharbati Gehu is a premium wheat variety native to Madhya Pradesh, recognized with a Geographical Indication (GI) tag for its distinctive quality. Haayat FPO works with member farmers in the Sehore region to promote authentic Sharbati cultivation, aggregate harvests with proper grading, and connect producers to buyers who value GI-certified wheat at premium rates.

Serving farmers across Sehore district from our hub at Bhairunda (Nasrullaganj) — Village Nehrugaon, Post Rithwar, Pin 466331.

What We Offer

  • GI-tagged Sharbati wheat cultivation support
  • Quality grading and aggregation at the FPO hub
  • Premium market linkages for authentic Sharbati produce
  • Farmer training on Sharbati-specific best practices
  • Traceability from farm to buyer for GI authenticity

Member Benefits

  • Premium prices for authentic GI-tagged wheat
  • Collective brand strength of Haayat FPO Sharbati supply
  • Quality assurance builds long-term buyer relationships
  • Recognition of Madhya Pradesh's heritage wheat variety

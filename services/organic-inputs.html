Overview

Consumer demand for organic produce is growing, and natural farming reduces long-term input costs while improving soil health. Haayat FPO supports members who want to transition to or continue organic farming — providing organic inputs, training on natural farming methods, and connecting organic producers to buyers willing to pay premium prices.

Serving farmers across Sehore district from our hub at Bhairunda (Nasrullaganj) — Village Nehrugaon, Post Rithwar, Pin 466331.

What We Offer

  • Organic fertilizers, bio-inputs, and natural pest management products
  • Training on composting, vermicompost, and jeevamrut preparation
  • Natural farming practice demonstrations in the field
  • Organic certification process guidance
  • Market linkages for organic produce buyers

Member Benefits

  • Premium prices for certified organic output
  • Reduced chemical input costs over time
  • Healthier soil and safer food for farming families
  • Support from FPO community of progressive organic farmers

How It Works

1

Register interest in organic farming program at the hub

2

Attend training session and receive starter inputs

3

Follow organic practices with periodic FPO field support

4

Sell organic produce through FPO buyer linkages

Overview

Haayat FPO aggregates demand from 700+ member farmers across Sehore district to procure certified agricultural inputs in bulk. By cutting out unnecessary middlemen, members receive genuine products at prices significantly lower than local retail — with quality verified at our Bhairunda hub before dispatch.

Serving farmers across Sehore district from our hub at Bhairunda (Nasrullaganj) — Village Nehrugaon, Post Rithwar, Pin 466331.

What We Offer

  • Certified seeds for soybean, wheat, chana, and seasonal crops
  • Organic and bio-fertilizers for long-term soil health
  • Micro-nutrients, crop protection, and plant growth regulators
  • Bulk group purchasing — members save up to 18% on input costs
  • 100% genuine products sourced from authorized distributors

Member Benefits

  • Lower input cost per acre for small and marginal farmers
  • Timely availability before sowing season at the FPO hub
  • Product authenticity guaranteed — no spurious or adulterated stock
  • Advisory support on correct dosage and application timing

Overview

Middlemen often reduce what farmers actually earn from their harvest. Haayat FPO connects member farmers directly to institutional buyers and processors — with transparent digital weighing, fair grading, and payments transferred quickly to bank accounts. Members have seen procurement rates up to 12% higher than local market prices.

Serving farmers across Sehore district from our hub at Bhairunda (Nasrullaganj) — Village Nehrugaon, Post Rithwar, Pin 466331.

What We Offer

  • Direct procurement of soybean, wheat, and chana
  • Transparent digital weighing — no hidden deductions
  • Fast payment transfers to farmer bank accounts
  • Linkages with institutional processors and bulk buyers
  • Price transparency and seasonal rate announcements at the hub

Member Benefits

  • Better realizations compared to local mandi middlemen
  • Instant weightment records for every transaction
  • Reduced post-harvest distress selling at low prices
  • Collective bargaining power of 700+ farmers

How It Works

1

Register your harvest intent at the FPO hub after crop maturity

2

Bring produce to the collection point or request pickup (where available)

3

Digital weighing and quality grading in your presence

4

Payment credited to your registered bank account

Overview

Good farming starts with understanding your soil and crop needs. Haayat FPO provides science-backed advisory services tailored to the agro-climatic conditions of Sehore and surrounding tehsils — helping farmers make smarter decisions on fertilizers, crop selection, and pest management rather than relying on guesswork.

Serving farmers across Sehore district from our hub at Bhairunda (Nasrullaganj) — Village Nehrugaon, Post Rithwar, Pin 466331.

What We Offer

  • Soil health testing and nutrient deficiency analysis
  • Crop disease and pest diagnosis with treatment recommendations
  • Seasonal weather advisories for sowing and harvest planning
  • Crop rotation and intercropping plans for better yields
  • Field visits and demonstrations for member farmers

Member Benefits

  • Reduce wasteful fertilizer use and input costs
  • Higher yields through crop-specific recommendations
  • Early detection of disease saves entire crop cycles
  • Localized advice suited to Madhya Pradesh farming patterns
